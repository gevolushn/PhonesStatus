**Last updated:** [вкажіть дату]

## Overview

PhonesStatus ("the App", "we", "us") is a tool that connects to Google Sheets on your behalf to read and update spreadsheet data that you authorize. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how it is protected.

## Information We Access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the App requests the following scope:

- **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets`** — allows the App to view, create, edit, and delete data in Google Sheets spreadsheets that you explicitly connect to the App.

We do **not** access any other Google data (Gmail, Drive files outside Sheets, Contacts, Calendar, etc.) unless separately and explicitly stated.

## How We Use Your Information

Data accessed via the Google Sheets API is used solely to:

- Read spreadsheet values you choose to connect
- Write or update spreadsheet values as instructed by you through the App's interface
- Display the resulting data back to you within the App

We do not use your Google Sheets data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core functionality described above.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google account data or spreadsheet content with third parties, except:

- When required by law
- With service providers strictly necessary to operate the App (e.g. hosting provider), who are contractually bound to protect your data

## Google API Services User Data Policy

PhonesStatus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking Access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access immediately stops the App from reading or writing to your spreadsheets.
